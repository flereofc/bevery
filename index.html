What is inside

Everything lives in one app

The website is for getting Bevery. The app is where the games, the people, and the workshop live.

Play in a click

Games open in their own play window the moment you press play. No per-game installs, no waiting around.

Publish your own

Write a game in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, upload one file, and everyone on Bevery can play it.

Make your avatar

Build a 3D look from hair, hats, shirts, and faces. Your avatar follows you everywhere on Bevery.

Play with friends

Find players by username, send friend requests, and keep up with what your friends are playing.

Earn and spend coins

Earn coins while you play, then spend them in the shop on new looks for your avatar.

Security that keeps up

A hardened game sandbox, protected accounts, and a security-focused 0.7.0 release that everyone should install.

Release notes

What's new in 0.7.0

A smaller, steadier update with a serious security pass under the hood.

Latest release

0.7.0

Beta

24 October 2026
Windows · Linux · Android

Update over your existing install. Your account, games, friends, and coins stay put.

Security

Install this one

Important hardening, updated dependencies, and stricter validation around account and game data. Every existing installation should update.

Interface

A cleaner fit

Improved text sizes and spacing, a more compact rank panel, better mobile navigation, and refined buttons, focus states, and tap feedback.

Desktop

Steadier on desktop

Updated Windows and Linux builds, fixed desktop networking, corrected game stats permissions, and removed noisy startup errors.

Mobile & fixes

Better on phones

Updated Android build with larger touch targets, no input zoom, smoother scrolling and modals. Game uploads also grew from 5 MB to 10 MB.
