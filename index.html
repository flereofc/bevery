What is inside

Everything lives in one app

The web is for getting Bevery. The desktop app is where the fun happens.

Play in a click

Games open in their own play window the moment you press play. No installs per game, no waiting around.

Publish your own

Write a game in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, upload one file, and everyone on Bevery can play it.

Make your avatar

Pick hair, hats, shirts, and faces to build a 3D look. Your avatar follows you everywhere on Bevery.

Play with friends

Find players by username, send friend requests, and keep up with what your friends are playing.

Earn and spend coins

Earn coins while you play, then spend them in the shop on new looks for your avatar.

Light and simple

The app starts fast and stays out of the way. Your account is protected by Google-level sign in security.
